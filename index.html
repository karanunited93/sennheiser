Driver technology

Three ways to move air inside an ear

Indian retail listings lead with driver type and count, for example "2 DD + 2 BA".12 These are the three main driver types.

Dynamic (DD)

A voice coil moves a cone-shaped diaphragm. Known for physical, impactful bass and a warm, natural blend. Diaphragm mass can soften fast transients.18

Balanced armature (BA)

A tiny pivoting reed drives a diaphragm through a pin. Strong on detail, tight transients and instrument separation. Bass is heard more than felt.18

Planar magnetic

A flat diaphragm moves evenly in a magnetic field. Combines detail with better bass texture, controls distortion well, and needs more power.18

Hybrids and crossovers

Multi-driver hybrids use crossovers to send each frequency range to a specialised driver. A poorly designed crossover creates audible artefacts at the handoff points.18 Examples from Indian listings: Kiwi Ears Quartet (2 DD + 2 BA), Quintet (1 DD + 2 BA + planar + piezo) and Astral (1 DD + 6 BA).12

Wired IEM

Single-ended 3.5mm, or 4.4mm balanced on many modern players.23

The Android 48kHz issue

Android has resampled USB audio to 48kHz, so a 192kHz stream is downsampled and even 44.1kHz CD-quality audio is upsampled.19 Android 14 added an optional bit-perfect USB mode, which device makers must enable.20 Android Authority argues that 48kHz resampling is high-fidelity enough for most listeners.21

Output impedance: the 1/8 rule

A common rule is that a source's output impedance should be at most 1/8 of the headphone's impedance, so 32Ω headphones want 4Ω or less. Some laptop outputs sit at 50Ω or higher, and Sonarworks measured frequency-response errors of up to 7 dB in the worst cases.24

4.4mm balanced

The 4.4mm Pentaconn plug is standardised under JEITA RC-8141C (2016). It adds a fifth conductor for a separate ground, which the older 2.5mm balanced plug lacks, and has been the norm on modern players and amps since about 2018.23

The AKM fire

On 20 October 2020 a fire hit Asahi Kasei Microdevices' plant in Nobeoka, Japan, which made AKM's audio DAC and ADC chips. Shortages of some AKM parts continued into 2021.22

Wired vs wireless

Bluetooth carries less than a CD

Uncompressed CD audio needs 1,411 kbps. Bluetooth codecs carry less than that.25

Bitrates in kbps, linear scale. LDAC runs at three settings.25 AirPods use AAC at up to 320 kbps.26

LDAC is not lossless

Sony's LDAC carries up to 24-bit/96kHz but compresses the high frequencies. At 330 kbps it is below CD quality, and it drops bitrate automatically when the connection weakens.25

AirPods and lossless

Apple Music's lossless tier does not reach AirPods, because they stream over AAC at up to 320 kbps.26

What "hi-res" means

The Japan Audio Society set 96kHz/24-bit as the minimum for hi-res products in 2014.27 Audibility is disputed. An AES meta-analysis found a small but statistically significant ability among trained listeners to tell hi-res apart; critics note this is only slightly better than chance.27

Reading frequency graphs

Targets, rigs and the limits of a squiggle

Squiglink is a creator-run database where reviewers, collectors and companies publish frequency-response measurements for thousands of headphones and IEMs.30

Relevant to the pitch

Harman's 2024 in-ear preference study ran its blind tests on modified Sennheiser MOMENTUM in-ears fitted with MEMS microphones.28

Harman 2024: two kinds of listener

72% · fuller bass
28% · leaner, brighter

32 Harman employees aged 21–50 rated curves blind on a 100-point MUSHRA scale. 72% preferred fuller bass with balanced treble. The other 28%, older on average at 39.3, wanted about 2 dB less bass and 1–2 dB more treble. The Harman IE 2019 target and the SoundGuys curve tied statistically, at 65.69 and 65.97.28

Cross-database comparisons

The accuracy of cheap IEC 711 clones against official GRAS rigs is unknown, and comparing graphs from different databases is inherently error-prone.29

Insertion depth

Reviewers often align graphs to the 8kHz resonance peak, but a couple of millimetres of depth shifts that peak and changes how the treble looks.29

Seal and unit variation

A badly seated IEM under-reports bass, and no two units leave the factory identical, so one measurement may not represent the product.29

Eartips

The cheapest upgrade in the hobby

Swapping eartips, known as tip rolling, can change an IEM's sound noticeably at low cost.31

Silicone

Easiest, most comfortable fit, easy to clean and sweat resistant. Tends to emphasise treble.31

Foam

Moulds to the ear for better isolation. Accentuates bass and tames brightness, but fit takes trial and error.31

What changes the sound
  • Wider bores lift the treble31
  • Seal sets bass and isolation31
  • Insertion depth shifts overall balance31
